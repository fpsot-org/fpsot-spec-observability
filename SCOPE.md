# Scope

This Working Group develops the FPSOT observability specification.

The specification covers how software records logs, traces and metrics, and how a public body keeps, protects and destroys those records: the log inventory and its schema, the record model on OpenTelemetry, the names, identifiers and timestamps of records, clock synchronization, the events of the audit stream other than the identity and access events, trace propagation, metrics and the health of the log pipeline, business events for process mining and their export to OCEL 2.0, output formats and transport to a log store, the minimization of personal information and keyed pseudonyms, retention profiles, legal hold, destruction and transfer records, and tamper evidence with Merkle trees, signed checkpoints and witnesses. It includes example records and the scripts that check them.

It does not cover the list and content of identity and access events, which the FPSOT identity and access specification sets, the choice of log products, security monitoring and incident response, a public transparency log of signed records, or legal advice.

Any changes of Scope are not retroactive.
