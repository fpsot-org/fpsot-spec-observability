= Retention, legal hold and destruction
include::_fpsot-terms.adoc[]

These rules set how long a log store keeps records, and how it destroys them.
A record that a public body holds is a document of that body.
In Québec, a body may destroy its documents only under its retention schedule.
A job that deletes old logs therefore destroys documents of the body.
So it needs to leave a trace of what it destroyed.

== Retention

[[OBS-100]]
*OBS-100.* A log store MUST let the operator set a retention period for each stream, in two tiers.
In the online tier, the records are ready for search.
In the storage tier, the records are out of search, but can come back on request.
A component and a log store MUST NOT fix a retention period in their code.
A log store MUST NOT destroy a record before the end of the period that applies to it.
The laws of each jurisdiction differ, so only the operator can set the period.

[[OBS-101]]
*OBS-101.* A log store MUST ship the retention profile `ca-qc-floor`, and MUST apply it when the operator sets no other.

[cols="2,1,1",options="header"]
|===
|Records |Online tier |Storage tier
|Each stream |90 days |180 days after the online tier
|Records tied to a security incident |2 years |None
|===

The 180 days of the storage tier follow the 90 days of the online tier, for 270 days in all.

The operator MUST set a longer period where a law or its retention schedule asks for one.
A log store MAY ship other named profiles.
Control `AU-11` of the {abbr-cgcd-first} sets these values: 90 days online, 180 in storage and 2 years for incidents.
The link:https://www.canada.ca/en/government/system/digital-government/online-security-privacy/cyber-security-guidance-policy/event-logging-guidance.html[Event Logging Guidance] of the Treasury Board of Canada Secretariat sets almost the same values for cloud services.
The profile therefore meets both as a floor, and other laws can only make it longer.

[[OBS-102]]
*OBS-102.* Each retention rule of a log store MUST name the rule of the retention schedule that it applies.
Examples are a rule of a Québec calendrier de conservation or a federal disposition authority.
A log store MUST record that reference with each destruction, under OBS-104.
An auditor can then check each destruction against the schedule that allowed it.

== Legal hold

[[OBS-103]]
*OBS-103.* A log store MUST let an authorized role place a {term-legal-hold} on records.
A log store MUST NOT destroy the records under a hold until a role lifts it, whatever their retention period.
A hold MUST be able to cover the records of one object, of a time range or of a stream.
Each hold, and each lift of a hold, MUST create an audit event under OBS-043.
The operator places a hold for an open access request, a complaint, an incident or a lawsuit.
Federally, section 4 of the link:https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-83-508/section-4.html[Privacy Regulations] asks for such a hold until the requester has used up their recourse.

== Destruction

[[OBS-104]]
*OBS-104.* Each destruction run of a log store MUST create an audit event with the `EventName` `org.fpsot.observability.record-batch.destroyed`.
The event MUST give the stream, the time range and the number of records.
It MUST also give the retention rule under OBS-102 and the latest checkpoint of OBS-112.
It MUST NOT hold the content of the destroyed records.
The event proves that a destruction followed the schedule, without keeping what the schedule asked to destroy.

[[OBS-105]]
*OBS-105.* A log store that moves records to another format or medium MUST keep a transfer record.
The transfer record MUST give the method, the date and the number of records.
It MUST also give a hash of the records before and after the move.
The Québec act on information technology asks to document a transfer, in articles _to be verified_.
A hash before and after shows that the move changed nothing.

[[OBS-106]]
*OBS-106.* Before it starts, each export for process mining MUST state its purpose and its retention period.
It MUST also state the date on which its pseudonym key ends.
The custodian MUST destroy the key on that date, under OBS-093.
Without an end date, an export would stay personal information forever.
